# AI-Assisted Contributions

AI-assisted and substantially agent-produced contributions are welcome. This project
evaluates relevance, correctness, evidence, maintainability, and review cost rather than
trying to infer which tool produced a change.

## Human responsibility

Before anything is published to this repository, a human contributor must:

1. read the complete final diff and public submission text;
2. understand the intended scope, material choices, and risks;
3. review the supporting evidence and reported check results, with unverified work stated
   explicitly;
4. confirm the contribution contains no private data, credentials, or material they lack
   the right to submit; and
5. remain available to review feedback and correct the work.

After that review, an agent may perform the mechanical steps of opening or updating an
issue or pull request. AI use does not require disclosure. Autocomplete, drafting,
translation, and extensive generation are held to the same outcome and accountability
standard.

## Unacceptable automation

Do not use an unattended agent to publish issues, pull requests, review replies, or other
project communication. Do not mass-submit speculative changes, fabricate sources or test
results, mark unresolved feedback as addressed, or use alternate accounts to evade a
maintainer decision.

Templates and checkboxes are not substitutes for reading the work. A contributor must be
able to explain why the change belongs, the basis for its material choices, and what
remains unverified. An agent may perform detailed verification, but its results must be
reported accurately.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close low-quality, misleading, bulk, out-of-scope, or unreviewable work
without providing a line-by-line critique or proving that AI produced it. Repeated
autonomous submission, spam, misrepresentation, or evasion may lead to reporting and
blocking through the hosting platform.

Grammar, accessibility, and translation assistance are not evidence of low-quality work.
Apply this policy to observable contribution behavior, not writing style or assumed tool
use.
